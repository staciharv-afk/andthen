After the service

The talking stops sooner than you'd expect.

For a week, everyone shares what they remember. Then people go back to work, and it gets harder to bring them up. The stories you heard in the receiving line are usually the first to fade.

At the service

One QR code on everything you print.

Every page comes with its own QR code. Busch can print it on the programs and memorial cards and set a sign by the guest book. Guests scan it with their phone camera, answer one question and add a photo if they have one. There's no app to download and no account to make.

Your QR code is ready when your page is.Download it from your page as a print-ready image or printable card.
Every kind of memory

Whatever form the memory takes, it has a place here.

Most of what people have isn't a written tribute. It's a voicemail they never deleted or a recipe card in her handwriting. Guests can add any of it from their phone, and it all sits together on one page.

Questions families ask

The short answers.

What does it cost?

One payment of $149 unlocks everything. Nothing to renew.

Do we need it set up before the service?

It helps. Start it a day or two before so the QR code can go on the programs. If the service has already happened, you can still send the link to everyone who was there.

How does the QR code work?

Each page has its own code. Guests point their phone camera at it, and it opens to a question about the person you're remembering.

Does everyone need an account?

No. Guests scan or tap the link and add a memory. Only you sign in.

Who can see it?

You decide. Anyone with the code or link can view the page, and you approve what gets added.

Can we keep everything?

Yes. The page stays live, and you can download every photo, video, voicemail and story at any time.
