Why now

The best things said about someone usually come too late.

People save their stories for the eulogy. By then, the person they're about can't hear them. A living eulogy gathers those stories now, so your person gets to read them, hear them and laugh at them with you.

How it works

Start it now. Your family keeps it after.

Start the page

Add their name and a photo. It takes about ten minutes, and you can do it from the bedside.

Invite everyone

Text one link to family, old friends and coworkers. Nobody needs an account, and people far away can add from anywhere.

Read it together

Read new memories out loud or play the videos and voicemails for them. Some families put the page up on the TV.

Keep it

After they're gone, the page stays. Your family keeps every story, and people can keep adding on birthdays and anniversaries.

Different questions for everyone

Including the person it's about.

Everyone gets a question that fits how they know them. Your person can add to the page too, by typing or by recording a story in their own voice for the grandkids.

Every kind of memory

Whatever form the memory takes, it has a place here.

Most of what people have isn't a written tribute. It's a voicemail they never deleted or a recipe card in her handwriting. People can add any of it from their phone, and it all sits together on one page.

Voicemails

The ones still saved on someone's phone.

Audio recordings

Old interviews, a toast at a wedding, a song they sang.

Videos

Birthday clips and the moments someone happened to be filming.

Photos

From phones, albums or a quick picture of an old print.

Spoken stories

Tap record and tell it out loud instead of typing it.

Written stories

A few lines or a few pages.

Handwritten notes

Cards and letters in their handwriting, photographed and kept.

Recipes

The recipe card everyone asks about, with the story behind it.

Links

A song, an article or anything online that reminds you of them.

Questions families ask

The short answers.

What does it cost?

Reserve Care families get half off: one payment of $74.50 instead of $149. Nothing to renew.

Is this only for after someone dies?

No. Most of all, it's for now, while your person can read it. The page stays afterward, and people can keep adding.

Can they add to it themselves?

Yes. They can type a story or record one in their own voice. Someone can also add it for them.

What if they can't read it on their own?

Someone can read it out loud or play the videos and voicemails for them, at the bedside or on a TV.

Does everyone need an account?

No. People tap the link and add a memory. Only you sign in, and you approve what gets added.

Can we keep everything?

Yes. The page stays live, and you can download every photo, video, voicemail and story at any time.

They should get to hear what everyone would say about them.
