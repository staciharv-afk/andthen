Questions families ask

The short answers.

What does it cost?

Free for Schoedinger families. There's nothing to pay, now or later.

Does everyone need an account?

No. The people you invite open the link and add a memory. Only you sign in.

Who can see it?

You decide. Pages start as invite only: anyone with the link can look, and only people you invite can add.

When should we start it?

Whenever you're ready. Some families start before the service so people can add to it that week. Others start months later.

Can we keep everything?

Yes. You can download every photo, video, voicemail and story on the page at any time.

Does it stay up?

Yes. The page stays live, and people can keep adding on birthdays and anniversaries.
